How to Play

1

Read the Trivia Question

Each equation has a trivia question. The answer is always a number that you need to place in the equation.

2

Place Numbers in Blanks

Click a number from the pool at the bottom, then click a blank space in an equation to place it.

3

Match Column Totals

Numbers in each column must add up to the target shown at the top. This is the key constraint!

4

Complete All Equations

Make sure each equation is mathematically correct, then submit to win and see the confetti!

💡 Pro Tips

Start with the answer: Place the trivia answer in its equation first to anchor your strategy.
Use column targets: The column totals help you narrow down which numbers can go where.
Need help? Tap Hint on an equation to reveal its answer or its numbers. ✓ Check shows its mark for one hint.
Earn stars: Every win earns 1 to 3 stars. Fewer attempts and hints earn more, and 3 stars is a perfect game. Check your stats to see completed puzzles, streaks, and stars.
Play with the keyboard: Select a blank and type a number to place it. Arrow keys move between blanks, Backspace clears one, Ctrl+Z undoes a move, Escape cancels, and Tab reaches the answer boxes.
Play offline: Install Sumwords from your browser's menu (Add to Home Screen on iPhone). After one online visit it plays offline, including the Daily puzzle.

Difficulty Levels

Every level has 5 equations with visible operators. Levels change the math, not the trivia, so any question can appear on any level.

Every trivia answer is a whole number from 2 to 400. Bigger figures are asked in thousands or millions, and the question says so.

More about the levels
  • Each equation uses two pool numbers, so the pool holds 10 numbers on every level before decoys are added.
  • Decoys are extra pool numbers that belong to no equation. You have to work out which ones to leave out.
  • Random picks one of the three levels for you.

What the Marks Mean

Press Submit to check your work. Each equation card gets one mark, and each column target gets ✓ or ✗. The trivia answer is never shown.

  • ✓ Correct: the numbers work with the operator and match the trivia answer.
  • ✗ Numbers don't work: the two numbers do not give the solution with that operator.
  • ✗ Answer doesn't fit the question: the numbers work, but the solution is not the trivia answer.
  • … Incomplete: a blank is still empty.
How column marks work
  • Each column ✓ or ✗ compares that column's total with its target at the top.
  • Submit marks equations and columns. It does not point to a single wrong number.

Features

🎯

Three Difficulty Modes

Easy, Medium, and Hard modes with increasing complexity. Plus a Random mode that automatically selects a difficulty level for you. Levels change the math (operators, number range and decoys), not the trivia, so any question can appear on any level.

📅

Daily Puzzle

One shared puzzle a day, always Everything and Medium. Everyone gets the same one, you can play it once per day, and you can share your result as Daily #2-12.

💡

Hint System

Tap Hint on any equation to reveal its trivia answer or its numbers. ✓ Check shows whether that equation is right for one hint, and is free while it is incomplete or already marked.

📊

Statistics Tracking

Every win earns 1 to 3 stars. Three stars is one attempt with no hints: each extra attempt costs 2 points and each hint costs 1. The win panel shows your time, and stats track completed puzzles, streaks, and stars.

📱

Mobile Optimized

Fully responsive design works great on phones, tablets, and desktop.

💾

Auto-Save Progress

Game automatically saves your progress. Resume unfinished puzzles anytime.

🎨

Clean Interface

Modern, intuitive design with color-coded feedback and visual polish.

↩️

Undo

Made a slip? Undo takes back your last placed or removed number. Use the Undo button or Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac). The history is kept in memory only, so a page refresh clears it.

⬇️

Install and Play Offline

Install Sumwords like an app and play offline after one online visit, Daily puzzle included.

How to install
  • Chrome, Edge or Android Chrome: use the install prompt, or open the browser menu and choose Install app.
  • iPhone or iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
