🎓 Welcome to Sumwords!

📝 How to Play

  1. Read the trivia question - The answer is always a number
  2. Place numbers in blanks - Click a number from the pool, then click a blank
  3. Match column totals - Numbers in each column must add up to the target shown
  4. Complete all equations - Make sure each equation is mathematically correct. Submit marks each one right, wrong or unfinished

💡 Tips

  • Start by placing the trivia answer in its equation
  • Answers are whole numbers from 2 to 400. A bigger figure is asked in thousands or millions
  • Use the column targets to narrow down number choices
  • Tap Hint on an equation to reveal its answer or its numbers
  • On a keyboard, press Tab to reach a blank and type a number to place it; arrow keys move between blanks, and Escape cancels
  • Made a slip? Undo (or Ctrl+Z) takes back your last placed or removed number. Check an equation's mark with its Hint row's ✓ Check, which costs one hint
  • Fewer attempts and hints earn more stars: ⭐⭐⭐ is one attempt with no hints
  • Check your stats to track your progress!
